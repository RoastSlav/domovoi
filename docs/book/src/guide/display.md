# Detail level

The web UI can show more or less detail. You choose a **detail level**, and it applies to the screens that offer one: the Operations list and each operation's page, the policy pages, the Knowledge pages (facts, fact and entity details, management paths) and the Experience search.

| Level                  | Shows                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Detailed** (default) | Everything: technical detail and metadata included.                                                    |
| **Standard**           | What matters. Technical detail and metadata are folded behind a labelled toggle with a count.          |
| **Essential**          | What matters and what needs you. Technical detail is folded; metadata is behind a "+N details" button. |

## What is always shown

Whatever the level, these are never hidden or folded:

- what is happening now, and what is waiting for you;
- approvals and the buttons to approve or deny;
- failures, refusals, unknown results, pauses, limits and stale data;
- what authority applies and what was recorded.

## Change it

- From the account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar): pick Detailed, Standard or Essential under **Detail level**, or open **Display settings**.
- In **Settings → Display**: set your default level, give a page its own level, read what each level does, or choose **Reset to Detailed**.

A page's own level wins over your default on that page. Removing it makes the page follow your default again. The Display settings are yours alone, and they stay available when a file manages the hub's other settings.

## Where it is kept

The choice is saved with your account on the hub, so it is the same in every browser and on every device where you sign in. Your browser also keeps a copy so the page does not flash at the wrong level while it loads; the hub's copy wins. If the choice cannot be read, the UI shows Detailed.

## What it does not do

The detail level only changes what is drawn. It never allows or blocks an action, and it does not change what is recorded, how long it is kept, or who can approve. Hidden details are one click away. See [Retention](retention.md) for what is kept and for how long.
